Progressive Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 July 2026

Progressive ("the app", "we") is a personal gym workout tracker. This policy explains what data the app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who runs Progressive

Progressive is operated by Navalo. Contact: utkarshg08@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Account information — if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, and profile picture from Google. If you sign in with email + password, we receive the email address and a hashed password. You may also choose "Continue without account", in which case no identity data is collected.
  • App activity you create — the workouts, sets, weights, reps, templates, exercise notes, body weight, plate increment, weight unit preference, avatar photo, and custom exercises you enter in the app. This is your fitness log; it lives on your device by default.
  • Cloud sync (optional) — when you are signed in, the app periodically uploads the JSON snapshot of your log to your private per-user row in our database (see next section). Sign out and reset from Settings stops the sync and clears the local copy on your device.
  • Diagnostic logs — the app writes local console output for debugging. We do not currently ship a crash-reporting SDK, and we do not receive automatic telemetry from your device.

Where your data goes

We use Supabase (privacy policy) as our backend. Supabase provides authentication and stores your snapshot in a hosted PostgreSQL database with row-level security — your row is readable and writable only by your own signed-in session. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest by Supabase.

Google authentication (if you choose it) is handled by Google under their own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it to train machine-learning models. We do not profile you across other services.

Legal basis (for users in the EU / UK)

We process your data on the basis of your consent (given by installing the app and signing in) and to perform the contract of providing the fitness-tracking service you signed up for. You may withdraw consent at any time by signing out and resetting your data from Settings.

Retention

Local data stays on your device until you tap "Reset All Data" in Settings or uninstall the app. Cloud data stays in your account until you request deletion by emailing utkarshg08@gmail.com — we will permanently remove your row within 30 days of a verified request.

Your choices

  • Use the app fully offline by choosing "Continue without account" on sign-in.
  • Sign out from Settings — clears the local session and wipes local data.
  • "Reset All Data" — nuclear option that wipes device data instantly.
  • Delete your cloud account — email us to remove your row from Supabase.

Children

Progressive is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us data, please email us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and bump the "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be announced in-app.

This privacy policy applies to the Progressive Android and web apps.
