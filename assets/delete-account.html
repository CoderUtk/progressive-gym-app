Delete Your Progressive Account

Last updated: 23 July 2026

Progressive ("the app") is operated by Navalo. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time using either of the options below.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

Fastest and fully self-serve.

  1. Open Progressive.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Reset all session and data.
  4. Confirm.

This clears every workout, template, profile field, and cached session on the device, and removes your data snapshot from our cloud backup.

Option 2 — Email request

If you no longer have the app installed, email utkarshg08@gmail.com from the address associated with your account with the subject line "Delete my Progressive account".

We will delete your account and all associated data within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record (email, name, user ID) in our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • Your workout snapshot in the user_data table (workouts, exercises, templates, profile fields, bodyweight, streaks, records).
  • Any uploaded profile picture.
  • All device-local data cleared automatically when you sign out or uninstall the app.

What is retained

  • Nothing. Progressive does not retain analytics logs, marketing data, or advertising identifiers tied to deleted accounts.
  • Standard server access logs (IP, timestamp) held by our infrastructure provider (Supabase) may persist for up to 30 days for security and abuse-prevention purposes, but are not tied to any user identity after account deletion.

Retention period

Data deletion requests submitted through either option above are processed within 7 days. In-app deletion via Reset all session and data is immediate.
